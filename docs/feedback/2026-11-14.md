# Feedback 2026-11-14 (build 0.1.0+64.3509f00, release zip, debug off)

One bundle: a practice session, then a 2 h timed race (Ligier JS P320, Motegi Full Road Course, 67 laps, one stop on lap 34),
plus two PresentMon captures (`sem` 11:36, `com` 11:48 local). No errors or warnings in the log. The driver saw nothing critical,
but did notice the target jumping (case 1); he never led the race. Clean race laps: 2.829 L/lap (sd 0.041) against a 2.892 L
baseline; he took the flag with ~2.8 L left. No recording (debug off), so the cases below are rebuilt as synthetic tests.

## 1. Target and colour flipping late in the race

Log: from 16:29 and again 16:54–16:58 UTC (laps ~41 and 55–58) `StateChanged` alternated red/blue every few seconds, with
`lapsRemaining` going 12.46 → 11.34 → 12.10 → 11.08 … and the target 2.75 ↔ 3.00 L. The 1.5 s hysteresis held some colour
changes but not the number (it is recomputed at 10 Hz). Cause: `LapsRemainingCalculator.ByTime` has two ceil() stages (the
leader's crossings until the clock runs out, then mine until he finishes). Within a lap LapDistPct runs up to ~0.02 lap ahead
of or behind a linear advance (measured on the 2026-11-13 Motegi recording: span 0.036–0.044 lap over 19 clean laps), so
pct + time/pace swings by ~0.04 lap, and a ceil() flips whenever the finish sits near a lap boundary. Fix: `TimedLapsHold`
keeps each stage's whole count while the value stays within 0.05 lap (~5 s) of it, follows each car across the line, and
restarts from the plain formula after a gap > 1 s, a clock going backwards, a new leader or missing inputs; with the clock at
zero the leader's count is exact. The pipeline keeps the history; the engine's old overload (tests, previews) still gives the
plain formula. Tests: `TimedLapsHoldTests` (own pace, leader and my own swing each reproduce the flip with the plain formula
and hold one finish lap; band, crossing, reset, NaN, no allocation), `Timed_laps_are_held_across_calls`.

## 2. "Stop required" for 6 s after taking the flag

Log: at 17:17:20 (my crossing on lap 67, the finish) `lapsRemaining` became 0.997 and the state NotFeasible (target 2.33 L)
until 17:17:28. Cause: after the checkered flag `AfterCheckered` returns 1 − pct, which right after my own crossing is the whole
cool-down lap. Fix: the pipeline notes my lap count when the flag comes out (`SessionState` 5); once it grows, or at once if I
am the leader then, `IFinished` makes the laps remaining 0 and the overlay inactive. Tests:
`Crossing_the_line_after_the_checkered_ends_the_race` (pipeline), `Finished_after_the_checkered_is_inactive` (engine, timed and
lap races).

## 3. Target 2732 L for 2 s at the green flag

Log: 15:15:13, `raw=3 targetL=2732 lapsRemaining=0.036` while still on the grid: `SessionTimeRemain` read ~0 for ~2.4 s. Fix:
the race clock cannot have run longer than the session, so a remainder below `length − SessionTime − 1 s` is unavailable
(no target) instead of "the last lap". Test: `A_race_clock_at_zero_before_it_could_have_run_is_unavailable`.

## 4. PresentMon (PERF-01): inconclusive

`FuelPace.Replay presentmon` said APPROVED, but the `sem` capture began with ~70 s of loading (`Composed: Flip`, a 22 s frame).
Steady state only (`sem` from 70 s, 143 s; `com` 300 s), iRacing capped at 120 fps in both:

| | sem | com |
|---|---|---|
| fps | 120.5 | 120.4 |
| frametime p99 / p99.9 | 9.75 / 10.70 ms | 9.76 / 10.61 ms |
| 1% low | 97.1 | 93.4 (96.2 %) |
| frames > 2× mean | 1 (0.06/1000) | 9 (0.25/1000) |
| present mode | Hardware: Independent Flip | Hardware: Independent Flip, all frames |
| iRacing GPU busy | 3.4 ms | 5.1 ms |

The overlay never pushed iRacing out of independent flip. The `com` hitches are ~35 ms of iRacing CPU (GPU normal), the same
signature as the single `sem` one, and each falls within 1.8 s of a session-info update in the FuelPace log (≈0.1 % chance by
coincidence), i.e. iRacing's own work. The +1.6 ms GPU busy (and +1.5 ms render-to-present latency) is consistent across the run
but cannot be attributed with one capture each. To repeat: same session, quit FuelPace from the tray for `sem`, start capturing
after ≥ 1 min on track, three pairs of 5 min.
