## 1. Laps iRacing counts as valid were invalid (`offtrack`)

Log: the capture ended `valid=0 total=13`; 9 of the 10 full laps had `offtrack` and 7 had `incident`. The profile had no
baseline, so the overlay showed `NoBaseline` (no target) for the whole race. In the race 9 of 18 laps had `offtrack`.
Recording (race): `PlayerTrackSurface == 0` for 0.15–1.1 s at the same two places almost every lap, pct 0.46 (~2.65 km,
~175 km/h) on 14 laps and pct 0.51 (~2.93 km, ~160 km/h) on 6, probably the exits of the two Lesmos; the qualifying
recording shows the same spot. iRacing gave 1x only twice (laps 4 and 10, the longest runs). The fuel is the same: clean
laps 3.210 L mean, offtrack-only laps 3.214 L. Cause: §6.3 invalidated a lap after 0.5 s of OffTrack surface, which also
reads on kerbs and run-off that iRacing tolerates. Decision (driver): a lap follows iRacing's leniency; if iRacing counts
it, so does FuelPace. Fix: the surface time is only measured (`OffTrackSeconds`), never a reason; an off that iRacing
punishes is an `incident`. Profiles saved before this lose `offtrack` on load, and outliers and the baseline are
recomputed (the driver's Monza capture recovers laps 14, 15 and 20: baseline of 3 laps, 3.234 L, low confidence).
Tests: `Offtrack_surface_without_an_incident_keeps_the_lap_valid`, `Offtrack_with_an_incident_is_invalid_by_the_incident`,
`Loading_drops_the_old_offtrack_reason_and_recomputes_the_baseline`, `Loading_a_profile_without_offtrack_keeps_the_saved_baseline`;
Zolder reference table updated (laps 19 and 24 stay invalid by `incident`, still 6 valid).

## 2. `reset` on a clean lap at the line

Log: `LapReasonTriggered reasonBit=16 value=-0.000009` right before the boundary. Recording: one frame at 242 km/h, on
track, Lap already incremented, with `LapDistPct = -0.000009` (also -0.000018 in qualifying). The detector reads any
pct < 0 as "not in world" (iRacing uses -1) and marked the closing lap as reset: race lap 5 (103.73 s, clean) and one
qualifying lap; probably also the `reset` of practice lap 12. Fix: `TelemetryFrameReader` reads a pct in
(-0.001, 0) as 0, the line, for every consumer; -1 is unchanged. Test: `TelemetryFrameReaderTests`.

Replay of the race recording after both fixes: 15 valid laps (all but the start, and laps 4 and 10 with the 2x), baseline
3.213 L, high confidence; before: 8 valid.
